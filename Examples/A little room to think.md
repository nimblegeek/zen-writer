# A little room to think

Good ideas rarely arrive fully formed. They need a little space, a little patience, and somewhere quiet to land.

Zen Writer is that place. No feed to scroll. No dashboard to manage. Just a blank page and the next sentence.

## Start with what matters

- Write one honest paragraph.
- Follow the thought a little further.
- Leave the polishing for later.

> Make something small. Make it yours. Then keep going.

Your words stay in **plain Markdown**. Save them wherever you like, open them in another editor, or turn them into an article when they are ready.

This is a local example document, not an article for publication.
